# Directly authored Hebrew documents: batch B

Five additional free-written continuing worlds, 70 original documents (five per level) and 40 grounded tasks. Six of the ten curated worlds belong to train, two to validation, and two to test, all group-isolated. Worlds here: model bridges, paper manufacturing, rock stratigraphy, autonomous aerial navigation and library budgeting. No external source texts were used. License CC0-1.0. These are research-stage data, not independently audited natural language prose.
